Runbook — Capricorn Policy Conversion Process

Live renewal-day conversion from the Legacy System (Pure) to the DCT Policy System

Document type: Operational Runbook (Post Go-Live) Teams: Aggne & Capricorn Version: 1.0 — Draft for client discussion Date: 02 July 2026

1Overview

This document outlines the policy conversion process for Capricorn policies on renewal, from the legacy system (Pure) to the DCT policy system. Conversion is performed at the time of renewal: predefined rules identify qualified policies, the SWIFT conversion tool validates the data and generates XML files, and ServerPolicy requests are triggered in DCT the same day the policy renews.

This is a continuous, live process — not a one-time migration. It runs for every renewal cycle, and this runbook defines the responsibilities, turnaround times, and escalation paths that keep it operating smoothly for all stakeholders over the coming year.

Tentative Turnaround Scale — Fix-By Deadline

Business rule: conversion data is collected 45 days before renewal (fixed). All corrections must be complete by the fix-by deadline so the remaining time is available to the underwriters. Default tentative turnarounds are based on the number of teams involved: 1 team → 2 days, 2 teams → 3 days, 3 teams → 5 days — these defaults assume fixes complete by 35 days before renewal (a 10-day fix window). Drag the deadline (e.g. to 30) and every turnaround time in this document adjusts proportionally.

2Key Participants & Ownership

Capricorn Team

Client — Data Owner

Responsible for data correction and validation in the source system.

  • Reviews pre-validation reports in the Capricorn Snowflake
  • Corrects flagged data in Pure (agencies, lengths, inconsistencies)
  • Fixes runtime-validation failures using the validation report within 3 days — corrected policies are collected in the next batch

Aggne — Conversion Team

Aggne — Process Owner

Oversees the conversion process end to end and owns validation reporting.

  • Identifies qualified policies and runs the conversion batch
  • Runs pre-validation and runtime validation; publishes reports
  • Analyses failures and routes them to the right owner
  • Triages error-in-response cases with the Policy Team; fixes code issues — turnaround 5 days

Aggne — Policy Team

Aggne — DCT Response Owner

Handles policy response issues in DCT.

  • Investigates invalid DCT responses (500 errors, timeouts)
  • Triages error-in-response cases jointly with the Conversion Team
  • Fixes DCT policy issues and resubmits — turnaround 5 days
Scope note: this process converts policies into DCT as quotes only — binding is not part of the conversion process. A successful conversion means a DCT policy number or quote number is received.

3Process Flow

Aggne — Conversion Team Capricorn Aggne — Policy & Conversion (triage & fix)
Same-day principle: qualified policies are converted in DCT on the day they renew in the legacy system. Pre-validation 7 days ahead exists precisely so that data corrections are complete before renewal day — corrections received late move the policy to the next available run.

4Conversion Process Steps

STEP 1Pre-Validation — 7 days Before Conversion

  • Identify policies eligible for conversion based on predefined qualification rules.
  • Perform pre-checks on legacy data to detect:
    • Missing agencies
    • Length issues
    • Any other data inconsistencies
  • Generate validation reports via the SWIFT conversion tool and publish them to the Capricorn Snowflake.
Capricorn Team's responsibility: review the pre-validation reports in the Capricorn Snowflake and correct the flagged data in their system within 2 days to avoid compliance issues on renewal day.

STEP 2Runtime Validation & XML Generation (Renewal Day)

  • Policies that pass pre-validation undergo runtime validation.
  • Mandatory fields, dropdown selections, and other required data are validated.
  • If validation fails:
    • The policy is bypassed from the conversion run.
    • The Aggne-Conversion Team shares the validation report and requests Capricorn to fix the data in their system.
    • Turnaround time for the data fix: 3 days
    • Once fixed, the policy is automatically collected in the next batch — no same-day rework is required.
  • Once validated, the system generates XML files for the qualified policies.

STEP 3DCT Request Processing & Response Handling

  • XML files are sent to DCT via ServerPolicy requests.
  • Responses are collected and analysed:
    • Success: a DCT policy number or quote number is received — the policy is successfully converted.
    • Invalid response (500 error or timeout): the Aggne-Policy Team investigates and resolves the issue within 2 days.
    • Error in response: a response is received but contains an error, meaning a process failed inside DCT. These are triaged jointly by the Aggne-Policy and Aggne-Conversion teams to determine the issue type:
      • Data issue → routed to Capricorn to fix via the validation report; the policy is collected in the next batch.
      • Code issue → the Aggne-Conversion Team fixes and resubmits within 5 days.
      • Policy issue → the Aggne-Policy Team fixes in DCT and resubmits within 5 days.
  • Note: this process creates policies in DCT as quotes only — binding is not part of the conversion process.
